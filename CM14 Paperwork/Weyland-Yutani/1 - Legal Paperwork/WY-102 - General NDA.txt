This [bold]Non-disclosure Agreement[/bold] is made effective as of March 30th 2124 by and between the Weyland-Yutani Corporation and the signatory regarding the property and information related to all operational procedures.

[bold]I. Confidential Information:[/bold] The term 'Confidential Information' means any information which is proprietary to Weyland-Yutani, whether or not owned or developed by Weyland-Yutani, which is generally known other than by Weyland-Yutani, which the signatory may obtain through any direct or indirect contact with Weyland-Yutani, regardless of whether specifically identified as confidential or proprietary. Confidential Information shall include any information provided by Weyland-Yutani concerning the business, technology, and information of Weyland-Yutani and any third party with which Weyland-Yutani deals, including, without limitation, business records and plans, trade secrets, technical data, product ideas, contracts, financial information, pricing structure, health discounts, computer programs, listings, and unknown wildlife, and all copyright and intellectual property. The nature of the information and the manner of disclosure are such that a reasonable person would understand it is confidential.

[bold]II. Protection of Confidential Information:[/bold] the signatory understands and acknowledges that the Confidential Information has been developed or obtained by Weyland-Yutani from investment of significant time, effort, and expense, and that the Confidential Information is valuable, special, and a unique asset of Weyland-Yutani which provides Weyland-Yutani with significant competitive advantage, and needs to be protected from improper disclosure. In consideration for the receipt by the signatory of the Confidential Information, the signatory agrees as follows:

[bold]III. Unauthorized Disclosure of Information - Injunction:[/bold] If it appears that the signatory has disclosed (or has threatened to disclose) Confidential Information in violation of this Agreement, Weyland-Yutani shall be entitled to an injunction to restrain the signatory from disclosing the Confidential Information in whole or in part. Weyland-Yutani shall not be prohibited by this provision from pursuing other remedies, including a claim for losses and damages.

[bold]IV. Non-Circumvention:[/bold] For a period of five hundred (500) years after the end of the terms of this Agreement, the signatory will not attempt to do business with or otherwise solicit any business contacts found or otherwise referred by Weyland-Yutani to the signatory for the purpose of circumventing, the result of which shall be to prevent Weyland-Yutani from realizing or recognizing a profit, fees, or otherwise, without the specific written approval of Weyland-Yutani. If such circumvention shall occur, Weyland-Yutani shall be entitled to any commissions due pursuant to this agreement or relating to such transaction.

[bold]V. Return of Confidential Information:[/bold] Upon the written request of Weyland-Yutani, the signatory shall return to Weyland-Yutani all written material containing the Confidential Information. the signatory shall also deliver to Weyland-Yutani written statements signed by the signatory certifying that all materials have been returned within one (1) day of receipt of the request.

[bold]VI. Term:[/bold] The obligations of this agreement shall survive until 03/30/2293 from the Effective date or until Weyland-Yutani sends the signatory written notice releasing the signatory from this Agreement.
